# Business Findings

## Executive Summary

The analyzed commercial dataset covers transactions from December 2009
through December 2011.

Key adjusted metrics:

- Gross sales: 336,438.69
- Operational cancellation value: 9,046.93
- Operational cancellation rate: 2.69%
- Adjusted net revenue: 327,391.76
- Commercial invoices: 837
- Identified customers: 624
- Countries represented: 20

Adjusted net revenue excludes two exceptional, unmatched cancellation
lines from the operational measure. The original records remain preserved
and available through observed net revenue.

## Monthly Performance

October 2010 generated the highest adjusted monthly revenue:

- Adjusted net revenue: 28,163.24
- Invoices: 51

Other high-performing months included:

- February 2011: 20,645.85
- November 2010: 20,387.52
- January 2011 adjusted: 19,484.24
- September 2011: 18,712.88

The source is a limited sample and should not be treated as a complete
representation of the retailer's historical revenue.

## Product Performance

The highest-performing merchandise products by adjusted net revenue were:

1. REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER: 6,590.99
2. WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER: 3,279.61
3. JUMBO BAG RED RETROSPOT: 2,904.29
4. ASSORTED COLOUR BIRD ORNAMENT: 2,547.68
5. PARTY BUNTING: 2,296.16

The leading product contributes approximately 2% of merchandise revenue,

## Customer Performance

The leading identified customers were:

1. Customer 14646: 27,889.30
2. Customer 12415: 14,022.92
3. Customer 18102: 9,731.77
4. Customer 17511: 8,423.54
5. Customer 14911: 6,044.90

The top customer contributes approximately 8.5% of adjusted revenue.

The top ten customers collectively contribute approximately 26.4%,

single customer.

## Geographic Performance

The United Kingdom dominates the sample:

- Adjusted net revenue: 249,716.40
- Identified customers: 568
- Approximately 76.3% of adjusted revenue

Other leading countries were:

- Netherlands: 28,515.49
- Australia: 14,022.92
- EIRE: 9,267.68
- France: 7,649.72
- Spain: 5,465.96

United Arab Emirates, Switzerland, and Greece contain negative net
revenue because their sampled records include cancellations without
corresponding positive sales.

## Customer Identification Coverage

Transactions with an identified customer account for:

- 82.83% of analytical rows
- 298,702.01 in adjusted net revenue

Transactions without a customer ID account for:

- 17.17% of analytical rows
- 28,689.75 in adjusted net revenue
- Approximately 8.8% of adjusted revenue

Unidentified transactions are included in total sales reporting but
excluded from customer-level segmentation.

## Day-of-Week Pattern

Tuesday and Wednesday generated the highest adjusted revenue in the
available sample.

However, this result should be interpreted cautiously. Saturday contains
only one invoice, confirming that the dataset is not balanced enough to
support strong conclusions about weekly customer behavior.

## Reporting Recommendations

The dashboard should contain:

1. Executive overview
2. Monthly revenue trend
3. Merchandise product performance
4. Customer performance
5. Geographic performance
6. Cancellation and exception analysis
7. Data-quality and coverage notes

Both observed and adjusted metrics should remain visible to preserve
transparency.
